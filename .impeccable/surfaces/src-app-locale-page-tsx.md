## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a luxury sourcing desk where fabric samples are objects you handle; it refuses the cream-serif textile brochure with a photo split and tile row.
OWN-WORLD: A realistically lit draped knit-cloth ground in pale stone tones (WebGL shader, static fallback), ivory sample cards with pinked-edge V-stitch knit swatches (procedural SVG texture, not photos) and brass hairline double frames, near-black ink, brass accent. High-contrast Didone display serif with one italic word; small tracked sans caps for labels; mono-free. Fabric colour is the only saturation. No handwriting fonts, no gradients on UI.
STORY: Visitor sees a prestigious knitter whose fabrics are tangible, understands knit/dye/finish from Zeytinburnu, and either explores fabrics or requests a quote. Unknown specs show a designed "Bilgi için ulaşın" slot.
